🤖 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE — FULL FACTUAL GUIDE

Artificial Intelligence = computer systems built to perform tasks that normally need human intelligence: learn, reason, solve problems, understand language, recognise images & create content.

📜 Origins & History

  • Term AI coined 1956 by John McCarthy; Alan Turing (UK/London) pioneer — 1950 Turing Test “Can machines think?”
  • Progress: early logic → Machine Learning → Deep Learning neural networks → Generative AI boom from 2022
  • Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial College London world‑leading AI research & ethics centres

🧠 Main Types Clearly Explained

  • Narrow / Weak AI: Built for specific jobs — voice assistants, search, chatbots, image recognition — all AI we use TODAY
  • General / Strong AI: Future concept — human‑level intelligence across EVERY task — does NOT exist yet
  • Super AI: Theoretical intelligence far beyond human ability — only long‑term research goal
  • Machine Learning: Learns patterns from data instead of rigid programming; Deep Learning uses layered brain‑like networks

💡 Real‑World Uses & Benefits

  • 🏥 Healthcare: faster diagnosis, cancer screening, new medicine discovery
  • 🏙️ London/Smart Cities: traffic flow, energy saving, public transport planning, safety
  • 💼 Business: data analysis, fraud detection, automated support, forecasting
  • 🎨 Creative: writing, art generation, music, design, video editing
  • 🌍 Environment: climate modelling, pollution tracking, renewable optimisation

⚖️ UK/London Rules & Ethics

  • UK AI Safety Institute based in London — global leader in safe AI development & risk rules
  • Focus: stop bias, protect privacy, reduce job disruption, fight deepfake misinformation, transparency
