🤖 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE — FULL FACTUAL GUIDE

AI = computer systems built to perform tasks that normally need human intelligence — learn, reason, solve problems, understand language, recognise images & create content.

📜 Origins & History

  • Alan Turing (London/UK) pioneer — 1950 Turing Test “Can machines think?” — father of modern computing & AI theory
  • Term AI coined 1956; evolved: basic logic → Machine Learning → Deep Neural Networks → Generative AI boom 2022+

🧠 Main Types Clearly Explained

  • Narrow / Weak AI: Specific tasks — voice assistants, search engines, chatbots, image recognition — all AI we use TODAY
  • General / Strong AI: Human‑level all‑round intelligence — DOES NOT EXIST YET
  • Machine Learning: Learns patterns from data automatically; Deep Learning layered networks inspired by human brain
