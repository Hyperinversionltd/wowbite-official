🤖 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE — COMPLETE FACTUAL GUIDE

Artificial Intelligence = computer systems designed to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence — learning, reasoning, problem‑solving, perception, language understanding & creativity.

📜 History & Origins

  • Term “Artificial Intelligence” coined 1956 by John McCarthy at Dartmouth College conference, USA
  • Early foundations: Alan Turing (London/UK) — Turing Test 1950, “Can machines think?” — pioneer of computing & machine logic
  • Progress: early logic programs → machine learning (1990s‑2000s) → Deep Learning / Neural Networks (2012 onwards) → Generative AI explosion 2022+
  • London & UK universities (Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial College London) world‑leading in AI research & ethics

🧠 Main Types of AI Explained

  • Narrow AI (Weak AI): Built for one specific task — voice assistants, search engines, image recognition, chatbots, recommendation algorithms — most AI we use today
  • General AI (Strong AI): Hypothetical future AI with ability to understand, learn & apply knowledge across ANY intellectual task like a human — does not yet exist
  • Super AI: Theoretical intelligence far exceeding human capability in all fields — long‑term research concept only
  • Machine Learning (ML): AI subset — systems learn patterns & improve automatically from data without exact step‑by‑step programming
  • Deep Learning: Advanced ML using layered Artificial Neural Networks — powers ChatGPT, image generation, speech translation, self‑driving tech
